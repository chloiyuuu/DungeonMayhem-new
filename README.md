# Dungeon Mayhem

## The Story
The kingdom is in peril! The beloved Princess has been kidnapped and dragged into the deep, treacherous depths of the **Dungeon of Mayhem** by the evil Dungeon Lord. You are the kingdom's last hope. As a brave adventurer, your job is to descend into the darkness, battle hordes of monsters, and fight your way through **10 grueling floors**. Slay the Dungeon Lord and save the Princess!

## About the Project
**Dungeon Mayhem** is a turn-based RPG dungeon crawler built in Java. It features a polished Java Swing Graphical User Interface (GUI), a class-based party system, scaling enemy difficulty, and an interactive system of saving and loading player progress.

The application has been recently migrated to use **Maven** for easy build management and execution.

## Features
- **Class Selection**: Choose your starting hero from three distinct classes: Knight, Archer, and Rogue. Each has unique base stats and abilities.
- **Turn-based Combat System**: Manage HP and MP. Use basic attacks and unique class skills to slay your foes.
- **10 Floors of Danger**: Progress through increasingly difficult enemies ending with a Final Boss fight on Floor 10.
- **Save/Load System**: Progress is automatically saved whenever you clear a floor. You can seamlessly resume your game by hitting "Continue" on the Main Menu.
- **Shop System**: Spend your gold between battles to recruit new party members!
